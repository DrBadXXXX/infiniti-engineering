Everything a collector needs

🎶

Discogs Integration

Search by artist and title or scan a barcode. GrooveNotes pulls in cover art, tracklists, musicians, production credits, and release details directly from Discogs. Import your entire Discogs collection via CSV.

💿

Physical Copy Tracking

Log every copy you own with format, condition grading, purchase price, catalog and matrix numbers, variant info, and storage location. Track pending orders and know exactly what your collection is worth.

🎧

Listening Sessions

Record every spin with the date, format, occasion, companions, a personal note, rating, and favorite track. Build a rich listening history that captures how your relationship with each album grows.

🎤

Concert Log

Keep a record of every show. Capture the artist, venue, city, date, band members, and your personal notes. AI-powered lookup can automatically fill in setlist and lineup details.

🌐

Community Notes

Share your listening and concert notes with the GrooveNotes community. See what other collectors think about the same albums. Reply, discuss, and discover new perspectives on the music you love. You maintain full editorial control over your community notes.

✨

Groovy AI Assistant

Chat with Groovy about your collection. Get album recommendations, explore music history, and gain insights into your listening habits. Each album also has its own AI chat for deep dives.

🔍

Album Lookup

Browse albums without adding them to your collection. Search Discogs or scan a barcode at a record shop to view full details before you buy. Add to your collection or wish list with one tap.

👤

Artist Profiles

See every artist in your collection with links to their albums, full Discogs discography, and concerts you've attended. Explore connections across your entire music library.
