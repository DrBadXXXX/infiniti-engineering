Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add wines to my cellar?

Tap the + button to add a wine. You can search the CellarTracker database by winery and wine name, or enter details manually. CellarTracker integration can fill in varietal, region, country, and related wine details.

Can I import my existing CellarTracker collection?

Yes. Go to the Import section and use CellarTracker CSV Import. Export your collection from CellarTracker as a CSV file, then import it into Cellar Somm. The app matches entries and populates wine details, bottle lots, storage locations, and purchase history when available.

How do I track bottle lots?

Each wine can have multiple bottle lots with quantity, bottle size, purchase price, store name, purchase date, storage location, and bin assignment. You can also mark lots as pending for shipments in transit and receive them later.

How do I log tasting notes?

On wine detail, tap Log Event. Choose event type, add notes, score (100-point scale), occasion, and companions. Notes are searchable from the Tasting Notes section. You can optionally share notes with the Cellar Somm community.

What bottle events can I track?

You can log bottles as Drank, Gave Away, Sold, or Missing. Each event records date, quantity, and optional notes. The tasting-note event supports detailed notes and scoring for consumed bottles.

How does Community Notes work?

You can share tasting notes publicly with the Cellar Somm community. Other users with the same wine can view and reply if replies are enabled. You can delete replies and block users directly. Set a community username in Settings to participate. Content is moderated during posting, including checks for inappropriate language and potential plagiarism.

What does the AI Sommelier do?

The AI Sommelier can answer questions about your cellar, generate wine summaries with critic scores and drink windows, suggest meal pairings, and provide cellar insights. It requires your own Gemini API key (free tier available with Google).

How is my Gemini API key handled?

Your Gemini API key is stored in Apple Keychain on your device. Cellar Somm does not store your key on our servers. AI requests are sent directly from your device to Google Gemini.

How do I import wine reviews?

Use Review Import to parse professional reviews from PDF exports. Supported sources include Wine Advocate, Vinous, James Suckling, Jeb Dunnuck, Wine Spectator, and Decanter. Parsing quality depends on source formatting and PDF quality.

You are responsible for ensuring you have rights/subscriptions to access and use source content from third-party review sites.

How do I back up my data?

In Settings, create manual backups or use daily automatic backups. Backups are stored locally on your device. You can restore from any saved backup, and up to 30 backups are retained automatically.

After restoring: restore is replace-only. The restored inventory replaces current local inventory and syncs to iCloud/CloudKit. Keep other devices open and let sync finish before making further edits.

Important: deleting the app removes local backup files stored in the app container. Before uninstalling, use Backup Folder sharing/export to copy backups to iCloud Drive or another safe location.

For extra protection:

  • iOS/iPadOS: enable iCloud Backup in system settings to include app data in device backups.
  • macOS/Finder backups: create encrypted device backups via Finder for full-device recovery.

Does Cellar Somm sync across devices?

Yes. Cellar Somm uses iCloud to sync data across devices signed into the same Apple ID.

How do I import wines from an invoice?

Use Invoice Import to paste order text from purchase confirmations. AI extracts wines, store name, order date, and prices for review before adding items to your cellar.

How do I manage my subscription?

GrooveNotes uses an annual subscription. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time through your Apple ID settings on your device under Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.
